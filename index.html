Biography

I am a Tenure-Track Assistant Professor of Computer Science at Spelman College in Atlanta, Georgia. My research lies at the intersection of wireless security, Internet of Things (IoT), and agricultural technology.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from the University of Nebraska-Lincoln in 2025, where I was advised by Prof. Nirnimesh Ghose. Prior to my doctoral studies, I earned my M.S. from Changchun University of Science and Technology, China, and my B.Eng from Nnamdi Azikiwe University, Nigeria.

My work focuses on securing critical infrastructure in challenging environments—specifically Agricultural IoT (Ag-IoT), underground sensor networks, and autonomous systems. I combine physical-layer security with lightweight cryptography to build resilient systems.

🎯 Current Focus

I am establishing the Spelman Secure Ag-IoT Lab. I am actively looking for motivated undergraduate students interested in IoT, Embedded Systems, and Cybersecurity.
